Welcome to Alfazal Group

Alfazal Group is a leading transport company in Pakistan, managed by seasoned professionals with decades of experience in the industry. Our team of motivated experts is equipped to tackle challenges head-on and deliver results efficiently.


Committed to excellence, we strive for the highest standards of quality in our fleets, human resources, and services. With a strong focus on integrity and customer satisfaction, Alfazal Group has established itself as a benchmark for quality and reliability in Pakistan's transport sector.


Since its inception, Alfazal Group has been at the forefront of providing specialized logistics and security infrastructure. Our journey is marked by a consistent drive toward operational excellence and the development of tailored solutions for high-risk industries — from explosive transportation to seismic operations support for the oil, gas, and mining sectors.
